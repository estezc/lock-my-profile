Questions

Is Lock My Profile free?

Yes. You can install it and use every feature for free.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It has no servers and no account. Your settings, the hash of your password and the activity log stay in your browser’s local storage.

Can someone just turn it off?

While the profile is locked, Lock My Profile blocks Chrome’s internal pages, including the extensions page, so it can’t be switched off from there. It is built to stop people who use your computer; someone with full control of the computer could still remove it, so combine it with your computer’s account password for maximum security.

What if I forget my password?

Use the hint or the recovery question from the lock screen. If you set neither, remove and reinstall the extension: that deletes its data and lets you start again.

Does it work in incognito windows?

Yes, if you allow it: in chrome://extensions, open Lock My Profile’s details and turn on “Allow in Incognito”.

Which languages and computers does it work on?

Google Chrome on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS, in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese.
